03 — Engineering / 01

Wishbones shaped like wings.

The double-wishbone front axle uses teardrop-profile links that generate around 40 kg of downforce on their own. Behind the centre-lock wheels sit 408 mm discs, clamped by six-piston monobloc calipers.

A wing taller than the roof.

The swan-neck rear wing is the first on a production Porsche with a drag reduction system. Flatten it on the straights at the press of a button; under emergency braking it snaps upright as an airbrake.

Four litres. Zero turbos.

03 — Engineering / 04

The radiator moved in. The luggage moved out.

One large central radiator, lifted from the 911 RSR, sits where the front boot used to be. Its hot air exits through the bonnet nostrils and is steered around the cabin by roof fins — freeing the flanks for active aero.
